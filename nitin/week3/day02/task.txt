Q1. Star Schema Design (Retail Sales)

Fact Table: sales_fact - product_id - customer_id - date_id - store_id -
sales_amount (measure) - quantity (measure)

Dimension Tables:

Product Dimension: - product_id - product_name - category

Customer Dimension: - customer_id - customer_name - city

Date Dimension: - date_id - day - month - year

Store Dimension: - store_id - store_name - location

A retail star schema has a central sales fact table
connected to product, customer, date, and store dimension tables.

Q2.  Star Schema vs Snowflake Schema

Star schema is simple and fast but uses more storage

Pros: - Simple design, Fast queries, Less joins
Cons: - Data redundancy, More storage

Snowflake Schema: snowflake schema is more normalized and efficient in storage but increases complexity and query time.

Pros: - Less redundancy, Better organization
Cons: - More complex, More joins (slower queries)


Q3.  Degenerate Dimension

A degenerate dimension is a dimension attribute stored in
the fact table without a separate dimension table.

Example: - order_id - invoice_number
